Our story

Less, but better.

We started Thread & Co. with one rule: make clothes we'd actually want to wear every day. No trend chasing, no inflated prices — just good fabric, careful fit and designs that last.

Every piece is tested for a full year before it reaches the shop, and we'll repair anything you bring back.

Questions, answered

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 24 hours and arrive in 2–4 business days. Shipping is free over $75, otherwise $6.95.

What is your returns policy?

Return anything within 30 days, unworn, for a full refund. Return shipping is on us.

How do I find my size?

Use the size guide in any product's quick view. Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit.

Are your fabrics sustainable?

Our basics use organic cotton and recycled fibres, made in partner factories that pay fair wages.
